Passport Photo Size Guide — Requirements by Country

📅 2026⏱ 6 min read✍️ Mian Hassam
⚠️ Important: Requirements change. Always verify the official dimensions and specifications from your country's passport issuing authority before submitting your application. The information below is provided as a general reference only.

Passport photo requirements vary significantly between countries — both in size and in rules about background color, head position, and expression. Here's a general overview of common standards.

Common Size Requirements by Country

General Photo Requirements (Most Countries)

  • Taken within the last 6 months
  • Clear, in focus, with good lighting
  • Plain, light-colored background (no patterns)
  • Face directly toward the camera, eyes open
  • Neutral expression (closed mouth)
  • No glasses in most modern standards
  • No head coverings unless for religious reasons
  • High quality, minimum 300 DPI for printing

Tips for Taking a Good Passport Photo

  • Use natural light or a well-lit room — avoid harsh shadows on the face
  • Stand against a plain white wall or hang a white sheet behind you
  • Take the photo at eye level, not from above or below
  • Use portrait mode on your phone for a clear, focused shot
  • Crop tightly to show head and shoulders only
