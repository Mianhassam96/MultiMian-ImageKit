How to Convert PNG to WebP (Preserving Transparency)

📅 Nov 2026⏱ 5 min read✍️ Mian Hassam

For decades, PNG has been the undisputed standard for web graphics requiring transparency. However, PNG files are notoriously heavy, often weighing 500 KB to 2 MB for a single high-resolution asset. Converting your PNGs to WebP allows you to preserve 100% of the alpha channel transparency while cutting file size by 60% to 80%.

✨ Fact: Unlike legacy JPG (which replaces transparent pixels with solid white or black), WebP natively supports 8-bit alpha transparency with both lossy and lossless compression modes.

Lossless WebP vs. Lossy WebP with Alpha

When converting PNG to WebP, you have two compression options:

  • Lossless WebP: Mathematically identical to the original PNG. Exact pixel preservation with an average 26% byte reduction. Best for sharp geometric line icons and pixel art.
  • Lossy WebP with Alpha: Compresses the color channels using lossy prediction while keeping the alpha transparency layer crisp. Produces 70% to 80% size savings with zero visible quality loss. Best for illustrations, photos, and shadows.
